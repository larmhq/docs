---
title: "Get disruption"
authMethod: "bearer"
api: "GET /api/v1/disruptions/{id}"
description: "Returns a disruption with its timeline and affected components"
---

Requires `incidents:read` permission.

<ParamField path="id" type="string" required>

</ParamField>
